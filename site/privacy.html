Privacy

The short version: we keep what the app needs to work, use it only to run the app, and don’t sell it, show ads or track you. Questions or requests: hello@bandstand.info.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, the name your band calls you, email address, a scrambled form of your password (never the password itself), and your photo if you add one.
  • Your band’s content: songs, charts and every version of them, cues, setlists, rehearsals, the days you’ve marked off, proposals and votes, and a log of who changed what.
  • Your devices: if you turn on notifications, the address your phone’s browser gives us to send them to.
  • Billing: Polar’s ids for your band’s subscription and when it is paid until. Polar keeps the payment details; we never see a card.
  • Server logs: addresses and pages requested, kept briefly (a few weeks at most) to keep the service working and safe.

Who sees it

The members of your band see what’s in your band. We look at data only to run the service or when you ask us to help. The companies that help us run it see only what their part needs:

  • Hetzner (Germany): the server and its backups.
  • SMTP2GO: sends the app’s emails (sign-up, invites, password resets).
  • Polar: takes payments, as merchant of record.

Cookies

Two, both needed for the app to work: one keeps you signed in, one remembers which band you’re looking at. No analytics, advertising or third-party cookies.

How long

As long as your account or band exists. Nightly backups are kept for two weeks. When you ask us to delete your account or band, we do, and it drops out of the backups within two weeks.

Your rights

Ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and we will; wherever you live, you have the rights the GDPR gives. Bandstand isn’t meant for children under 13.
